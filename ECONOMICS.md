# Economics

## What is a Converted Lead Worth to Credex?

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. Assumptions:
- Average deal size: $2,000 (one-time credit purchase)
- Gross margin on credits: ~30% = $600 per deal
- Repeat purchase rate: 40% within 6 months
- LTV per customer: $600 + (40% x $600) = **$840**

Conservative estimate: **$600-840 LTV per converted customer.**

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Channel

| Channel | Est. Users/Month | Conversion to Purchase | CAC |
|---------|-----------------|----------------------|-----|
| Organic Reddit/HN | 200 | 2% | $0 |
| LinkedIn outreach | 50 | 4% | $0 |
| Credex email list | 500 | 5% | $0 |
| Paid Twitter ads | 300 | 1.5% | ~$45 |
| SEO (month 6+) | 400 | 2% | ~$10 |

**All primary channels are $0 CAC** — this is a content/community play,
not a paid acquisition play. That's the point of building a free tool.

## Conversion Funnel Math
1000 audit completions
→ 250 email captures (25% — value shown before ask)
→ 50 high-savings audits >$500/mo flagged for Credex (20% of captures)
→ 15 consultation calls booked (30% of flagged)
→ 8 credit purchases (53% close rate on consultation)
→ $4,800 revenue per 1000 audits ($600 avg deal x 8)

**Revenue per audit completion: $4.80**
**Break-even: Tool costs $0 to run (Supabase free, Vercel free, Resend free tier)**

At 1000 audits/month, this tool is immediately profitable with $0 marginal cost.

## What Makes This $1M ARR in 18 Months

Target: $1,000,000 ARR = ~$83,000/month revenue

Working backwards:
- Average deal: $2,000 one-time + repeat = $2,800 LTV
- Deals needed: 83,000 / 2,800 = **~30 deals/month**
- Consultations needed (53% close): ~57/month
- High-savings audits needed (30% book): ~190/month
- Email captures needed (20% flagged): ~950/month
- Audit completions needed (25% capture): **~3,800 audits/month**

**3,800 audits/month = 127/day.**

This is achievable if:
1. Credex email list drives 1,000 audits in month 1
2. One viral HN/Reddit post drives 500-2,000 audits in a week
3. SEO compounds from month 3 onward
4. Referral loop from shared audit URLs drives 20% of traffic organically

## Key Assumptions That Could Be Wrong

- **Deal size $2,000** — could be lower ($500) if customers are early-stage
  solopreneurs. Adjust: need 6x more conversions for same revenue.
- **5% conversion from Credex email list** — could be 1-2% if list is cold.
  Adjust: warm the list with value emails before pitching.
- **30% consultation booking rate** — optimistic. Could be 10-15%.
  Adjust: improve the Credex CTA copy on high-savings audits.

## What Triggers a Pivot

If after 60 days:
- Audit completion rate is high (1000+) but email capture is below 15%
  → Fix: improve value proposition, show savings number before email gate
- Email capture is good but consultation booking is below 5%
  → Fix: Credex CTA is not compelling, rewrite or add social proof
- Everything works but deal size averages below $500
  → Fix: target larger teams (50+ employees) with higher AI spend
