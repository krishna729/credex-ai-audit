# User Interviews

## Interview 1
**Name:** Harsh Kumar Vyas
**Role:** Final-Year CSE Student & MERN Stack Developer
**Company stage:** Student / Fresher 
**Location:** Hyderabad, India

**Responses:**
1. Tools used: Claude (paid)
2. Monthly spend: $20/month
3. Overspending concern: No — "it's doing a great job"

**Direct quotes:**
- "Claude — $20/month — no it's doing a great job"

**Most surprising thing:** A final-year student is paying $20/mo 
for Claude out of pocket — not a company budget. This means even 
individual developers see enough value to pay personally, not just 
teams.

**What this changed in my design:** The tool should serve individual 
developers too, not just startup teams. Added "solo developer" as a 
use case option in the form.

## Interview 2
**Name:** Shivam Sharma
**Role:** Software Engineer at Kotak (Banking/Fintech)
**Company stage:** Large enterprise employee
**Location:** Bhopal, India

**Responses:**
1. Tools used: Claude
2. Usage: ~2 hours/day
3. Overspending concern: No — "very helpful in increasing overall 
productivity"

**Direct quotes:**
- "No. It is very helpful in increasing overall productivity"

**Most surprising thing:** Enterprise employees are using personal 
AI subscriptions for work tasks — their company is not paying for 
it. This is a hidden cost that companies don't track at all.

**What this changed in my design:** Added messaging around "shadow 
AI spend" — employees paying personally for tools their company 
should be providing. This is a real GTM angle for Credex.

## Interview 3
**Name:** Atul Gupta
**Role:** CTO at RoboKriti.India Pvt Ltd, Product Developer
**Company stage:** Early-stage startup
**Location:** Jabalpur, India
**Education:** IIIT

**Responses:**
1. Tools used: Mostly free versions
2. Monthly spend: Very low / near zero
3. Overspending concern: Not applicable — deliberately avoids paid 
plans

**Direct quotes:**
- "Very less use of AI tool, most of the free version will use"

**Most surprising thing:** A CTO of an actual startup is 
deliberately staying on free tiers — not because of lack of 
awareness but conscious cost control. Early-stage CTOs are 
extremely price-sensitive and need to see clear ROI before 
upgrading.

**What this changed in my design:** Added a "free tier maximizer" 
section — show users how to get the most from free plans. For 
CTOs like this, the audit should show exactly what they'd gain by 
upgrading, with a clear cost-benefit breakdown.
