## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no trial period. You get the full audit
instantly at no cost. We capture your email after showing you the
results — never before.

**Q: How do you make money if this is free?**
A: This tool is built by Credex, which sells discounted AI credits
to startups. If your audit shows significant savings, we may reach
out about how Credex can help you save even more. If you're already
spending optimally, we tell you that honestly and don't push anything.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every number in our audit engine is sourced from official vendor
pricing pages, verified weekly. Sources are published in our open
GitHub repo. If you spot an error, open an issue.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Your email and company name are never shown on the shareable
audit URL. Only tool names and savings numbers are public. We don't
sell your data. Ever.

**Q: What if I disagree with a recommendation?**
A: Good. The audit is a starting point, not gospel. Every
recommendation includes the reasoning so you can evaluate it
yourself. A finance-literate person should be able to read our
logic and agree or push back with specifics.
